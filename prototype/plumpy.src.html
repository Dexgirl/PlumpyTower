      How to play

      Stack real Chog NFTs into a tower. Reach the line, knock the villain off its perch and free the caged Chog. 50 levels, 50 real Chogs to free.

      👆 Basics🎯 PERFECT🐸 Squad🍔 Rugs🍪 Boosters😈 Villains📈 Market🌍 Twists⭐ Stars🥊 Fight🗼 Endless💜 Own📲 App⌨️ Controls
      👆 The basics
      • Plumpy flies left and right holding your next Chog. Tap the stage (or the Drop button, or Space) to let go.
      • Land on the plate, then on each other. Reach the dashed line to win the level.
      • Each level gives you a set number of drops (the bar at the top shows them). In practice levels they're free.
      • If the tower falls, that try is over: tap Build a new tower and go again.
      🎯 PERFECT and combos
      • Land dead centre on the Chog below for a PERFECT. The ring under Plumpy turns green when you're on target.
      • PERFECTs pay the most crumbs. PERFECTs in a row build a COMBO (x2, x3…) worth even more.
      • Plumpy flies faster as the tower gets taller.
      🐸 Your squad
      • Every Chog you own is a block. You start with 3, and every level you clear frees one more.
      • Pick who drops next in the Squad strip. Each Chog drops once per round, then the whole squad goes again.
      • Head → top shape. Caps and hats are flat (easy to stack on). Plain heads are round (things slide off). Horns, crowns and thorns are pointy (nothing sits on them, so save them for the top).
      • Rarity → weight. Common to Legendary: rarer Chogs are heavier. Side is the way it leans.
      • Powers come from Base, Aura and Accessory: ⭐ Legend · ☢️ Radioactive goo (sticks) · 🦴 Bone heavy · 🌬️ Windproof · 🔥 Hot (melts ice) · 🧊 Chill (ignores sell-off shaking) · 👑 Royal (wider PERFECT zone) · ☁️ Floaty · 🪶 Soft landing · 🪙 Golden (double crumbs).
      • Open Squad on Home to see every Chog, its powers and its OpenSea page.
      🍔 Feed a rug
      • Feed a rug (or press F) fattens the Chog you picked: wider and heavier, so the tower holds steadier. Up to 6 per Chog.
      • Each level gives you a few rugs, and bellies reset every level. On a Diet day level there are none.
      🍪 Crumbs and boosters
      • You earn crumbs on every drop, more for PERFECTs and combos, plus for squishing babies, surviving market events, quests, clearing levels and the boss fight.
      • Spend them on boosters in the dock: 🍔 Rug (30) one more rug this level · 🩹 Glue (50) your next drop sticks where it lands · 🧲 Magnet (40) next drop's PERFECT zone is twice as big · 🛡️ Hedge (60) the next market event hits at half strength.
      • All the crumbs you've earned set your Rank at the top of Home.
      😈 Villains and their babies
      • Every level has a real NFT villain guarding a cage, riding a pet that carries your plate.
      • Each time you stack, the villain spits babies onto your screen. They steal crumbs and leave messes on your view. Tap them to squish them and get crumbs back. (You can turn them off in Settings.)
      📈 The $CHOG market
      • The little chart at the top is $CHOG. Market events hit the tower: 📈 Pump (moon gravity) · 🐋 Whale buy · 📉 Sell pressure (shaking) · 🟥 Rug pull (the plate tips) · 🧊 Liquidity freeze (ice) · 🌪️ FUD storm (a giant gust).
      • A warning flashes first, so hold your drop until it passes. The goal bar says when the next one is due.
      • Many levels end with a final candle: once you reach the line, hold the tower through one last event to break the cage.
      • Today's mood on Home changes every day and decides which events show up.
      🌍 Level twists
      • Ice (everything slides) · wind gusts · moon gravity · a moving or tilted plate · fog · the Bear biting the plate smaller · Diet day (no rugs) · the Bear picks who drops · a drop timer · only rare Chogs allowed.
      • Flag levels: reach the height, then put a pointy Chog on top.
      • The level card shows the twist before you start.
      ⭐ Stars, modes and the bounty
      • Up to 3 stars: clear it, use few drops, land enough PERFECTs. The level card shows the targets.
      • Clear a level to unlock 🎰 Degen (wilder market, double crumbs) and ⏱️ Speedrun (Plumpy lets go after 3 seconds) for it.
      • Today's bounty on Home: one level pays triple crumbs.
      🥊 The boss fight
      • Reach the line and Plumpy climbs the tower, smashes the cage, and the freed Chog fights the villain.
      • Tap when the shrinking ring meets the target ring. PERFECT and GOOD hits do damage and earn bonus crumbs. Skip fight is there if you just want the win.
      🗼 Endless and quests
      • Endless: no goal, just height. Climb through the zones; your best height shows on Home.
      • Quests: 3 new ones every day for crumbs, plus the Road to the Purple Moon.
      💜 Own the real Chogs
      • Every Chog in the game is a real Chog Genesis NFT on Monad. "Own the real #N" opens it on OpenSea.
      • The game never asks for your wallet. No approvals. Ever.
      📲 Play it like an app
      • iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share ⬆︎ (on newer iPhones it sits inside the ••• menu), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
      • Android (Chrome): tap Get the Plumpy app on Home, or the menu ⋮ then Install app.
      • Computer (Chrome or Edge): the install icon at the right of the address bar.
      • Your progress saves by itself. The home-screen app keeps its own save, so if you played in the browser first, use Settings → 💾 Save code to bring it across.
      ⌨️ Controls and settings
      • Tap / Space / Enter drop · F feed a rug · Esc pause.
      • Settings: sound, music, voices (talk, babble or off), baby crawlers, and Plumpy's story again.
